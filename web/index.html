XTE Label Push

Draw a 250×122 four-ink label and send it to a Poshiji PSJ-213 over Web Bluetooth. No server, no account.

Needs Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS, served over HTTPS or from localhost. Safari, iOS and Firefox have no Web Bluetooth. After the push the panel flickers for about 20 seconds: that is the refresh, not a fault. The tag must not be connected elsewhere (bluetoothctl disconnect on Linux).
